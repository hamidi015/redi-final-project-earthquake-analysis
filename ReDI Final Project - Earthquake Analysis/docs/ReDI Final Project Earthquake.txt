3. Project Roadmap (Step‑by‑Step)
This is the exact workflow you will follow.

Step 1 — Define the Research Questions
Your project will answer these professional, clear questions:

Main Research Question
How have global earthquake patterns changed over time, and what factors influence earthquake severity?

Sub‑questions
How many earthquakes occur each year globally?

Are earthquakes becoming more or less frequent over time?

Which regions experience the most earthquakes?

What is the distribution of earthquake magnitudes?

What is the relationship between earthquake depth and magnitude?

What are the strongest earthquakes recorded in the dataset?

These questions are perfect for ReDI because they are:

Clear

Measurable

Easy to visualize

Easy to explain in a 5‑minute presentation
